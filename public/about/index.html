About chat-widget

`chat-widget` is a lightweight embeddable chatbot for websites.

What it is for

It gives site visitors a floating chat assistant that can be configured with your own instructions, theme, links, and contextual info.

Pages

  • Home - minimal setup and live demo.
  • Advanced setup - full configuration reference.
  • Widget UI - runtime widget interface.

Routes

  • Canonical: /embed.js, /widget.html, /.
  • Legacy compatibility: /v1/* and /v2/* forward to canonical paths.
